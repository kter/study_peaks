🗑️ Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. If you'd like to delete your Global Study Peaks account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

What gets deleted:
  • Your account profile information
  • Study session history
  • All data associated with your Google account in our system

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to us with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include:

  • The email address associated with your Google Sign-In
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
📧 Request Account Deletion

Processing Time

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

Alternative: Sign Out

If you just want to stop using the app without deleting your data, you can simply sign out from the app's profile menu. Your data will be automatically removed after 24 hours of inactivity.
